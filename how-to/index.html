How to use Board Game Log

Short guides for the things people do most. Tap a title to open it.

The screenshots use made-up games and players.

Add a game

Three ways, from the quickest to the most detailed.

  1. In the Games tab, tap Add game.
  2. Choose Just a name to type the game's name and save. You can add more later.
  3. Or choose From an archetype for a starting shape, such as a score sheet, a team game or a co-op game. Pick one, give it a name and adjust what it tracks.
  4. Or choose From scratch to say how the game is won and what to track: scores by category, counters, dice, turns, teams or hidden roles.
  5. Tap Save. The game appears on the Games tab.

Good to know. You can change a game later with Edit game. Matches you already recorded keep what they have.

Quick log: a match you already played

Three taps, then the details if you want them. The sections fold: Players and Result start open, the rest start closed.

  1. Open the game and tap Quick log.
  2. In Players, pick the group (or let the app make one) and add the players in table order.
  3. In Result, tap the winner, or the result that fits the game, and save.
  4. Optional: open Colors to give each player a color, and When to set the date and the start and end time.
  5. Optional: tap Add details for points, counters, awards, how it ended and notes. They are grouped (Players, Result, Time, Game, Rules, Notes) and each group folds. The end of the match is a date and time; the time played is worked out from it.

Good to know. Play again on a finished match starts a new one with the same group and players.

Record a match and play it live

For when you want the score, the counters, the dice and the clock while the game is going. You can prepare the match first and start the clock later.

  1. Open the game and tap Record a match. Choose the group, the players, when it starts (leave it, or pick an earlier time if the game already began) and, if you like, their colors, the game's options and a photo. For a game with dice or rounds, Track lets you switch them on or off for this match. The match is saved as Ready to start.
  2. When you begin to play, tap Start match. The clock starts then. A match that is ready waits on the game page and on the Games tab until you open it.
  3. Score as you go: a +X for each category, counters with − and +, and one button for each condition the game has.
  4. For games with dice, tap Roll to roll on screen, or Enter roll to type what the real dice showed.
  5. Use End round between rounds, Undo to take back your last action and Pause to stop the clock.
  6. When someone reaches the goal an alert asks Finish match or Keep playing. Finish suggests the result from what was played; change it if it is wrong, choose the date and time it ended if it was earlier, and save.

Good to know. A match in progress survives a closed app. Resume it from the card at the top of the Games tab, or Abandon it to keep it out of your stats.

See your stats

Every game has its own stats, for one group or for everyone you play with.

  1. Open the game and tap Stats.
  2. The Leaderboard shows wins, win rate and matches played. Tap a player for streaks, last matches and head to head.
  3. Use Filters to look at one group or a range of dates.
  4. More pages appear as you add matches: scores, categories, counters, dice luck, time per turn and more.

Good to know. A match missing something a page needs? Add details to past matches in the Stats tab walks you through them.

Sign in (optional)

You do not need an account to keep your games. Sign in only to share with friends or to keep your games equal on every phone you use.

  1. Open the Settings tab and choose Account.
  2. Sign in with Apple or with Google.
  3. To keep your games on every phone you use, open Cloud and choose Upload my games. The app makes a backup first, uploads, checks both sides match and only then turns the sync on.

Good to know. Everything else works without an account. Sharing a group, a game or a match in progress needs one.

Share a group with friends

A group is the people you play with. Friends join with a code, a QR or a link.

  1. Open the Players tab and choose the group (or New group).
  2. Tap Invite people. You will see a code, a QR and a link; copy the code or show the QR.
  3. Your friend signs in, opens Join a group and types the code, scans the QR or opens the link.
  4. When they join, the app asks which player are you, so their matches and stats show as You.

Good to know. If a code ends up in the wrong hands, make a new one and the old one stops working.

Let friends follow a match live

Friends see the score from their own phone while you play, and can roll the dice if you let them.

  1. Start a match as usual (see Record a match and play it live).
  2. In the match, choose Share live and then Start sharing. Copy the link or show the QR code.
  3. Friends who have an account open the link and watch. They cannot change your score. Choose Stop sharing whenever you like.

Good to know. Only a match in progress can be shared, and sharing stops by itself 24 hours after the match ends.

The same person in two groups

If a friend plays in two of your groups, you do not have to merge them. Link them instead.

  1. Open the Players tab and open the person's page.
  2. Choose Link to a person in another group and pick the other profile.
  3. The person's page now offers This group and All groups. Each group keeps its own stats.
  4. To undo it, choose Unlink. Nothing is lost.

Good to know. Merge is only for a duplicate inside one group, for example the same friend typed twice.

Share a game, duplicate it or propose a change

A game you made can be shared with your groups, so their members can play it with the same rules, in matches of that group.

  1. Open the game, tap the … menu and choose Share with group…, then pick the group. Only you can share it, with as many groups as you like. Members can see the game and use it in matches of that group, but cannot change it or share it.
  2. A member who wants their own version taps Duplicate to get a copy they can edit and use with their own groups. They can have one copy of a game at a time, a copy cannot be copied again, and it says whose game it is based on.
  3. A member who wants the owner to change the rules opens View rules and taps Propose changes, edits and sends it with a message.
  4. The owner sees the proposals on the game, reads what changes and chooses Accept or Reject.

Good to know. You cannot take a group back once a game is shared with it. If the owner leaves the group, the game stays there as it was, but nobody can share it any further.
