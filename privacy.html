Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 17th, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Board Game Log ("the App") handles your data. The App is developed and published by YOURNAME ("we", "us").

1. Overview

Board Game Log is a local, device-based app for tracking board game sessions. We do not operate servers for this app, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Game sessions (players, scores, dates, duration, game type, scenarios).
  • Dice roll history (if you use the dice feature).
  • Player groups and player names.
  • Board photos that you choose to attach to a session.

This information is only stored on your device and is removed if you delete the App or erase its data. We do not have access to this information.

3. Camera and Photo Access

The App may request access to your camera and photo library so that you can take or select photos of your board or game setup. These photos are only used inside the App and are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers because we do not run any servers for the App.

4. Network Connections

The App may optionally allow local connectivity features (for example, a local web page used as a remote dice controller) over your local network. This communication happens only between devices on the same network and is not sent to us.

5. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not include third-party analytics SDKs and does not use any tracking technologies to profile users or serve advertising.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not contain content that is inappropriate for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with external social networks, advertising platforms, or other third-party services that would receive your data.

8. Your Choices

  • You can delete game sessions, photos, and other data from within the App at any time.
  • You can revoke camera or photo permissions in your device settings.
  • Deleting the App from your device will remove the data stored by the App on that device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
