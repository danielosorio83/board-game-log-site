Keep score of every game night.

Board Game Log is an iPhone app to record who played and who won, score a match as you play, and see how everyone is doing. It works on your phone, with no account.

What it does

For the games you really play, from the big box to the card game on the kitchen table.

  • Any game

    44 ready-made games from Catan to Yahtzee, or type a name and start. Add scores, counters, dice, teams and hidden roles when you want them.

  • Record in three taps

    Pick the group, add the players in table order, tap the winner. Points, time and notes can come later, or never.

  • Play live

    Score as you play with +X per category, counters, dice you can roll or enter, a timer, undo, and an alert when someone reaches the goal.

  • Your stats

    Wins and win rate, streaks, head to head, scores, dice luck, time per turn, and more once you have a few matches. Each group has its own, and a person who plays in several can be seen as one.

  • Catan, in depth

    Resources by roll, barbarians, Longest Road and Largest Army with their points, board photos and a scan of the board layout.

  • Friends, if you want

    Sign in to share a group, a game, or a match in progress. A friend can follow the score from their own phone and even roll the dice.

Yours first

Everything works without an account and stays on your phone. Backups are a file you keep. Sign in only if you want to share, or keep two phones in sync. There are no ads and no tracking.
